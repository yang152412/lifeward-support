# Lifeward Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Developer: Shichang Yang

Privacy contact: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/yang152412/lifeward-support/issues)

Lifeward is a self-management tool that helps people set usage allowances for selected apps. This policy explains how Lifeward handles and protects data.

## 1. Data We Process

To provide the core experience, Lifeward processes the following data on your device:

- app or category identifiers you select through the system picker;
- shared and individual allowances, protection status, and notification preferences;
- usage duration, allowance status, and recent usage records for selected apps;
- usage for up to 7 complete calendar days before protection and the dates on which a limit was reached;
- per-app temporary-access status, selected duration, daily count, and accumulated time;
- operational status such as whether required access is working.

Lifeward does not read browsing content, messages, photos, contacts, accounts, passwords, or keyboard input inside selected apps. The current version does not require an account.

Lifeward does not ask for, store, or analyze why you temporarily allow an app. Time reflections shown during cooling periods come from a built-in library and are not generated from your app content.

## 2. How We Use Data

This data is used only to calculate allowances, show usage, apply limits, calculate temporary-access time and uses separately for each app, send necessary alerts you allow, and show whether protection is working. When enough system data is available, Lifeward also uses the highest-use complete day among up to 7 days before protection as a historical baseline and estimates the reduction after protection. Temporary-access usage remains included in actual usage. This is an estimate, not directly observed time saved.

Historical Screen Time usage, hourly curves, the baseline, and estimated reduction are calculated and rendered only inside Apple’s Device Activity Report privacy sandbox. They are not written to the shared container or uploaded to the developer’s servers. The shared container stores only the protection scope, statistics start time, and date-level indication that a limit was reached, deduplicated by calendar day.

## 3. System Access

- Screen Time / Family Controls: lets you choose apps through the system privacy picker and enables usage monitoring and limits. Lifeward receives protected identifiers supplied by the operating system and cannot read content inside those apps.
- Notifications: used for low-allowance alerts, temporary access ending soon, and protection issues. Declining notifications does not disable core blocking.

You can turn all monitoring off inside Lifeward or remove one app from protection. This does not automatically revoke Screen Time authorization; revoke it separately in system settings if desired. Turning monitoring back on or adding an app again restores its saved rules, and usage while it was off or removed is not backfilled.

## 4. Sharing, Sale, and Advertising Tracking

Lifeward currently does not sell personal data, use cross-app advertising tracking, or share the local data described above with third parties. If third-party services or processing practices are added later, this policy will be updated first and any notice or consent required by law or platform rules will be provided.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Rules and dates on which a limit was reached remain locally on your device. Changing the protection scope or stopping protection resets the current impact period. Uninstalling Lifeward removes data in the app container. The operating system may include app data in backups according to your device and cloud-backup settings.

## 6. Security

Lifeward relies on operating-system sandboxing, permission controls, and protected APIs to reduce unauthorized access. No method is completely secure. Contact us using the method below if you discover a security concern.

## 7. Children and Teens

Lifeward is intended for people who can manage their own device usage. A parent or guardian should decide whether a minor may use the app according to local law and the family’s circumstances. Lifeward does not knowingly collect children’s account or content data.

## 8. International Transfers

The current version does not upload core data to the developer’s servers, so the developer does not initiate international transfers of that data. This section will be updated before cloud sync or another network service is introduced.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

You can review or change rules, turn monitoring or notifications off, revoke system access, or uninstall the app to remove local data. Where applicable law grants access, correction, deletion, objection, or complaint rights, contact us using the method below.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy when features or data practices materially change and provide notice in the app or as required by the applicable platform. The effective date will be updated with each new version.
